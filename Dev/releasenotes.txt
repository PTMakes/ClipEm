New Features
- Post-clip: ClipEm can keep recording for a few seconds after you press the clip hotkey, so you catch what's said right after. Turn it on in Settings > Recording. Your clip saves as usual, along with a "-extra.mkv" copy that has the extra seconds.
- In the clip editor, a clip with a post-clip copy opens ending at the hotkey press. Use Include extra, or drag the end handle, to keep the extra seconds.
- Clip naming lets you choose the post-clip copy's suffix.
- Release notes: update prompts show what's new, ClipEm shows them after it updates, and Settings has a Release Notes button with the notes for every version.

Improvements
- On Windows and Linux, OBS now stays fully out of sight: ClipEm's tray icon is the only one, and OBS's errors and messages appear as ClipEm notifications.
- Settings and the clip editor have their own icons.
- Settings' Clip Output/Devices tab now uses two columns, with Audio Devices beside the recording settings, so it fits without scrolling.

Fixes
- OBS no longer checks for missing files at startup, a dialog nobody could see with OBS hidden.
